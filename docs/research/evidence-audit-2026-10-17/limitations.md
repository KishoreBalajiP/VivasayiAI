# Limitations and blocked work

- Synthetic geometry, fusion, and chat results use analytic or author-defined labels; they establish implementation conformance only.
- The name matcher was tuned after errors were observed on the same 60-pair set; a held-out corpus is required.
- No independently labelled crop-loss parcels, field surveys, insurer assessments, or verified cadastral records are present.
- Live satellite cases were selected near documented events without verified crop, land cover, or damage labels. One harvest control false-supported loss, showing phenology risk.
- RAG baseline parity failed and the planned before/after experiment was stopped on 2026-10-17. No improvement claim is permitted.
- Live chat and document extraction were blocked by provider quota; no substitute mock results are reported.
- Human evaluation requires at least two qualified raters, blinded presentation, fixed rubric, disagreement reporting, and adjudication.
- Backend and frontend test/evaluation commands stalled in this audit before producing reports; exact cause remains to be diagnosed.

## Unsupported claims and required evidence

1. Real-world crop-loss or satellite damage-detection accuracy: independently labelled parcels, crop-calendar metadata, pre-registered windows, and a held-out test set.
2. Agricultural advisory correctness and safety: curated reference answers, expert agronomist raters in relevant languages, citation audit, abstention rubric, and live-model logs.
3. Land-document extraction accuracy: consented or public document images, double annotation plus adjudication, and field-level exact match/precision/recall/F1.
4. Fraud, ownership, duplicate, or cadastral decision accuracy: authorised records with independent adjudication and privacy-preserving sampling.
5. User experience benefit from follow-up logic: consented farmer study with Tamil materials and predefined outcome measures.
